Cookies worth waiting for

Why make cookies that take a whole day? It took years of scouring the internet, combining recipes, and testing methods — resting the dough, baking it right away, freezing dough balls, oil versus plant-based butter — to land on the soft chocolate chip cookie so many of us were raised with. After five years of plant-based baking, this is the recipe that consistently gets great feedback, whether I use regular or gluten-free flour.

  • 20cookies
  • 12–24 hrtotal rest
  • 30 minactive prep
  • 12–14 minbake time

Ingredients

Ingredients for bowl 1

  • All-purpose flour (or 1:1 gluten-free)250 g
  • Baking powder1 tsp
  • Baking soda3/4 tsp
  • Salt1/2 tsp
  • Dairy-free chocolate chips280 g

Ingredients for bowl 2

  • White sugar100 g
  • Brown sugar110 g
  • Water1/3 cup
  • Vegetable oil1/2 cup

Instructions

  1. Combine the flour and other dry ingredients mix together
    in a large bowl pour in chocolate chips and coat in flour then set aside.
  2. In a separate bowl, thoroughly mix the sugars, oil, and water, making sure there are no clumps.
  3. Slowly mix the dry ingredients into the wet ingredients.
    Becareful not to overmix
  4. Once combined, cover the bowl and refrigerate for 12–24 hours.
  5. Remove the dough from the fridge and preheat the oven to 350℉.
  6. Form the dough into approximately 20 balls, each weighing about 43 g.
  7. Place in preheated oven for 12–15 minutes, rotating half way through

Notes✪

For firmer cookies that hold there shape, after forming dough balls in step 6
place the cookie sheet in the freezer for approximately 10 minutes.
before placing them in the oven to bake.
Cookies can be stored in air-tight container for 5 days at room tempature.
